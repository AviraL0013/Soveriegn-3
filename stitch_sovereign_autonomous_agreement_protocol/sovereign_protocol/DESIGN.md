## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-conviction visual language for autonomous financial agreements, algorithmic collateral, and institutional capital protocol execution. It fuses the mathematical precision of high-frequency trading terminals with the permanence and physical authority of legal contracts printed on archival paper.

### Personality & Tone
- **Unyielding Authority:** Every element feels deterministic, auditable, and binding. There are no decorative flourishes or ambiguous gradients.
- **Institutional Rigor:** Clean geometric structure conveys solvency, protocol liquidity, and cryptographic finality.
- **Instrumental Density:** Prioritizes data legibility, state immediacy, and rapid visual parsing over decorative whitespace.

### Design Movement: Neo-Brutalist Institutional Fintech
The aesthetic employs architectural neo-brutalism refined for mission-critical financial software. Crisp, razor-sharp 1px and 2px near-black strokes frame high-density containers, anchored by hard zero-blur offset drop shadows, structured tabular telemetry, and stark, electric action states.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an archival paper-and-ink metaphor heightened by electric digital signifiers. Surfaces rely on high-contrast physical stratification rather than soft optical diffusion.

### Core Swatches
- **Canvas Base (`#F5F4EF`):** An archival, unbleached warm paper tone that reduces eye fatigue across marathon capital operations while establishing a tactile, physical contract baseline.
- **Card & Component Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity pure white sheets stacked over the canvas baseline, delivering maximum contrast for numerical figures and structural borders.
- **Ink & Architectural Bounds (`#111111`):** High-density near-black utilized for structural hairline borders, hard offset shadows, primary typography, and terminal-grade division lines.
- **Primary Accent (`#3155FF`):** Kinetic electric blue dedicated strictly to decisive operational triggers, primary balance routing, active state borders, and interactive focus rings.

### Semantic Tiers
- **Settled / Safe / Audited (`#00875A`):** Pure institutional emerald for verified smart contract logic, settled yields, positive delta values, and solvent collateralization ratios.
- **Elevated / Pending / Warning (`#D97706`):** Sharp amber for pending epoch confirmations, transaction mempool propagation, and approaching margin boundaries.
- **Liquidated / Breach / Terminal Fault (`#DC2626`):** Uncompromising crimson reserved for liquidation cascades, transaction failure states, signature rejections, and contract violations.

### Neutral Layering
- **Subtle Surface Tint (`#ECEAE2`):** Used for inset table headers, secondary metric trays, and inactive switch rails.
- **Muted Typography (`#5A5A57`):** High-legibility tertiary tone for metadata labels, contract timestamps, and column descriptors.

## Typography

Typography functions as visual engineering. Three distinct type systems operate in strict hierarchy to serve structural navigation, prose readability, quantitative analysis, and machine-level verification.

### Type System Breakdown
- **Display & Headlines (`Geist`):** Crisp, geometric sans with sharp mechanical cuts. Delivers uncompromising presence in viewport headings, card titles, and protocol agreement clauses.
- **Narrative & UI Body (`Inter`):** Selected for its hyper-neutral readability under dense operational loads. All financial numbers rendered in Inter must apply the OpenType feature tag `tnum` (Tabular Figures) along with `cv05` to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time balance streaming.
- **Telemetry, Hashes & Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to transaction hashes, cryptographic key digests, block heights, network gas rates, and uppercase micro-labels. Its deliberate character widths enforce visual alignment across nested lists and ledger streams.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a deterministic 4px baseline grid coupled with a column-based structural matrix. The interface rejects arbitrary fluid drift in favor of solid mechanical modules.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column rigid grid, `2.5rem` (40px) outer margins, and `1.25rem` (20px) gutters. Complex dashboards split into an 8-column transactional canvas and a 4-column dedicated execution ledger/action drawer.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. The secondary drawer stacks below the main viewport or docks as an expandable bottom-sheet console.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column grid with `1rem` page margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Cards collapse to full-bleed edge-to-edge containers with 1px side boundary strokes.

### Density Rules
- High data-density zones (spreadsheets, yield ledgers, contract terms) leverage `0.5rem` vertical cell paddings to maximize above-the-fold information volume.
- Interactive control groups and financial agreement sign-off modules expand to `1rem` and `1.25rem` padding boundaries to provide tactile assurance and zero mis-click tolerance.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system strictly disallows blurred drop shadows, ambient glow halos, and skeuomorphic gradients. Elevation is communicated solely through physical hard-edge offsets, razor strokes, and high-contrast plane superposition.

### Mechanical Elevation Tiers
- **Flat Ground (Level 0):** Background surface (`#F5F4EF`). Static structural dividers use a 1px solid stroke in `#111111` or `#D4D2C9`.
- **Raised Panel (Level 1):** Standard modules, transaction review cards, and parameter trays (`#FFFFFF`). Constructed with a 1px solid border (`#111111`) and a static hard shadow: `2px 2px 0px #111111`.
- **Interactive Floating / Focus (Level 2):** Hovered cards, open dropdown menus, modals, and active execution dialogs. Upgraded to a 2px solid border (`#111111`) with an exaggerated hard shadow: `4px 4px 0px #111111`.
- **Engaged / Pressed (Level 3):** Buttons and active tabs during click or execution translate physically down and right by `2px 2px`, reducing the shadow to `0px 0px 0px #111111`. This provides an immediate, tactile response mimicking heavy industrial mechanical switches.

## Shapes

The geometric philosophy of this design system is purely **Sharp (`roundedness: 0`)**. Every corner throughout the entire component ecosystem terminates at an absolute 90-degree right angle (`0px` border-radius).

### Geometric Logic
- **No Chamfers or Fillets:** Border radii remain universally `0px` across all inputs, badges, cards, dialog windows, and notifications.
- **Structural Integrity:** Right angles reinforce the legal, institutional authority of the protocol, anchoring figures to an immutable, grid-aligned structure.
- **Clipped Indicators:** Where status visual variety is needed (such as ledger tags), 45-degree angled corner cutouts (notch corners via CSS clip-path, 4px by 4px) are used exclusively for machine-level verification badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Execution Button:** Background `#3155FF`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #111111`, shadow `2px 2px 0px #111111`. Text is bold, medium tracking. On hover: shadow expands to `3px 3px 0px #111111` with a slight upward diagonal shift. On active press: translates `2px 2px`, shadow clears to `0px 0px 0px #111111`.
- **Secondary Structural Button:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#111111`, border `1.5px solid #111111`, shadow `2px 2px 0px #111111`. Hover background transitions to `#F5F4EF`.
- **Destructive / Liquidation Button:** Background `#DC2626`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #111111`, shadow `2px 2px 0px #111111`. Focus ring is a 2px offset solid `#DC2626`.

### Cards & Execution Trays
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #111111`, hard shadow `2px 2px 0px #111111`.
- Card headers utilize a bottom border of `1px solid #111111` accompanied by a subtle `#ECEAE2` background strip holding `label-caps` category metadata.

### Input Fields & Segmented Steppers
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border in `#111111`, inset padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Typography utilizes `JetBrains Mono` for currency input and cryptographic fields.
- **Focus State:** Border expands to 2px solid `#3155FF` with a hard outline offset `1px 1px 0px #3155FF`. No ambient blur.
- **Numeric Asset Inputs:** Right-aligned inline currency tickers (e.g., `USDC`, `ETH`) encased in a 1px solid `#111111` pill-box container with `#F5F4EF` fill.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Sharp rectangular tags with `label-caps` micro-typography.
- Border is `1px solid #111111`, background matched to state:
  - **Verified / Settled:** Background `#E6F4EA`, text `#00875A`.
  - **Pending / In Escrow:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`.
  - **Defaulted / Failed:** Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#DC2626`.
  - **System Static:** Background `#ECEAE2`, text `#111111`.

### Data Tables (The Protocol Ledger)
- Dense, zero-fluff data tables built for multi-million dollar capital verification.
- **Header Row:** Background `#ECEAE2`, border-bottom `2px solid #111111`. Column headers use `JetBrains Mono` at `10px`, uppercase with `0.08em` letter spacing.
- **Table Cells:** Top and bottom hairlines `1px solid #E2DFD2`. Numbers utilize `Inter` tabular figures (`num-table`) aligned strictly to the right. Hashes and addresses truncate middle (`0x4f...99bc`) in `JetBrains Mono` aligned left.
- **Row Hover:** Pure `#FFFFFF` elevates to `#F9F9F6` with a solid 2px left border strip in `#3155FF`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes:** Sharp 16px by 16px boxes, 1.5px solid `#111111`. When checked, solid `#111111` fill with a pure `#FFFFFF` geometric checkmark.
- **Segmented Radio Bars:** Enclosed in a 1px `#111111` border tray. Active segment snaps to solid `#111111` background with white text, casting a miniature `1px 1px 0px #111111` internal offset.

### Protocol-Specific Components
- **Agreement Signature Strip:** A dedicated terminal confirmation strip containing hash root, gas cap, and block expiry. Outlined with a 1px dashed `#111111` border on a `#ECEAE2` background, requiring a positive click-and-hold slide interaction to authorize capital movements.
- **Collateral Health Ratio Gauge:** Segmented linear metric bar separated into discrete blocks (not a continuous gradient). Segments shift abruptly from `#DC2626` (unsafe) to `#D97706` (moderate) to `#00875A` (fully collateralized), delineated by 1px vertical near-black division lines.
